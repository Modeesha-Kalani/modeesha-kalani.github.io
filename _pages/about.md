Hi, I am Modeesha.

I am an **Advanced Specialist DevOps & SRE** at [Pearson](https://www.pearson.com), based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. I have more than four years of hands-on experience in **AWS**, **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)**, **automation**, and **CI/CD**. I currently lead Pearson Lanka's largest infrastructure initiative (PDP/PIF) under the Developer Experience and Productivity (DXP) team, serving as the SME for AWS CloudFormation Managed Services Templates.

I hold an **MSc in Artificial Intelligence** from SLIIT (2025–2026) and a **BSc in Information Technology (Software Engineering)** from SLIIT (2019–2023). I am certified in **AWS Solutions Architect**, **AWS AI Practitioner**, **HashiCorp Terraform Associate**, and **GitLab CI/CD**.

## Research & Projects

My research project **HearMe** — a learning development platform for hearing-impaired children — was recognized by Microsoft, SLASSCOM, NBQSA, and the World Summit Awards. I founded and led the project in collaboration with Wickramarachchi Hearing Care Providers and the Sri Lanka Hearing Impaired Children Association.
