Hi, I am Modeesha.

I am an **Advanced Specialist DevOps & SRE** at [Pearson](https://www.pearson.com), based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. I have more than **five years** of hands-on experience in **AWS**, **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)**, **automation**, and **CI/CD**, and I am currently pursuing an **MSc in Artificial Intelligence** at SLIIT.

I lead Pearson Lanka's largest infrastructure initiative — **PDP/PIF** (Pearson Development Pipeline / Pearson Infrastructure Framework) — under the **Developer Experience and Productivity (DXP)** team. I serve as the **Subject Matter Expert (SME)** for **AWS CloudFormation Managed Services Templates**, working closely with engineering teams across the organization to deliver scalable, compliant, and efficient cloud solutions.

## Summary

Cloud and DevOps professional with a strong track record in platform engineering, infrastructure automation, and site reliability. I have progressed from **Intern Cloud Engineer** to **Advanced Specialist DevOps & SRE** at Pearson, owning critical platform epics, mentoring engineers through interviews and onboarding, and driving compliance and reliability across AWS accounts.

Beyond my industry role, I founded **HearMe**, an AI-powered learning platform for hearing-impaired children that earned national and international recognition — including **Microsoft Imagine Cup Sri Lanka Winner (2024)** and **Top 20 global semifinalist** at Imagine Cup 2024.
