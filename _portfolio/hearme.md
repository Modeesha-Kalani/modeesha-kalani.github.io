**HearMe** is a learning development platform designed for hearing-impaired children worldwide. I founded and led this project from February 2022 to January 2024.

The platform was developed in collaboration with **Wickramarachchi Hearing Care Providers** and the **Sri Lanka Hearing Impaired Children Association**.
